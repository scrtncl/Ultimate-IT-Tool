AI Technician

Ask a question about your network. The answer comes from real test results, and shows what is still unknown.

Sample conversation only. No AI is connected yet, and every result below is made up.

Conversation

The technician can run read-only tests. It cannot change anything by itself.

You

Why does my Wi-Fi disconnect every 10 minutes?

Technician

Ran 4 read-only checks: Wi-Fi link, router logs, interface counters, signal strength.

Your internet connection looks healthy, so the problem is probably on the Wi-Fi side. Here is what the tests showed:

  • Your laptop's signal is strong (-48 dBm).
  • The router's 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi shows 212 errors and a high retry rate on channel 6.
  • Your laptop dropped off the Wi-Fi 3 times in the last 30 minutes.

The most likely cause is interference or crowding on channel 6. I can't confirm that yet, because I haven't scanned for nearby networks. The findings panel lists what I'd test next.

You

Can you just fix it?

Technician

I can prepare a plan, but I can't change your router on my own. Changing the Wi-Fi channel is a higher-risk change, so it needs your approval and a backup first. I'd suggest running the channel scan before choosing a channel. Open Router Center to review the plan when you're ready.

Findings

Facts, likely causes, and unknowns are kept apart

What was measured

  • Internet, DNS, and router checks pass
  • 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi: 212 errors, high retry rate on channel 6
  • Laptop signal -48 dBm (strong)
  • 3 Wi-Fi drops in 30 minutes

Most likely cause

Interference or crowding on 2.4 GHz channel 6.

Medium

Based on test results only. It goes up or down as more tests run.

Other possibilities

  • A power-saving setting on the laptop's Wi-Fi adapter
  • Router restarting the Wi-Fi radio (nothing in the logs shows this)

Not verified

Nearby networks, the exact time of each drop, and the laptop's adapter settings.

Recommended tests

  1. Scan Wi-Fi channels for crowding
  2. Test the same laptop on a wired cable
  3. Line up the drop times with the router log

Possible fix (not applied)

Move 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi to a less crowded channel. Medium to high risk: needs a backup, your approval, and a rollback plan.
